Abstract. Large language models (LLMs) represent a breakthrough in natural
language processing, boosting performance in tasks such as text generation and
question answering. However, they face challenges such as hallucinations and
lack of access to updated information. The Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG) technique seeks to mitigate these problems by integrating external in-
formation retrieval into text generation, improving the accuracy and timeliness
of the answers. This work investigated several open-source and proprietary
embedding models and LLMs applied to the RAG technique, considering three
databases containing documents written in Brazilian Portuguese. The experi-
mental results demonstrated that the Multilingual E5 large and Gemma 2 9B
models obtained the best performance among the evaluated models based on
different evaluation measures.
